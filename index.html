    Understanding the Kp Index

    The Kp index measures geomagnetic activity on a scale from 0 to 9. Higher values indicate stronger auroral activity and visibility at lower latitudes.

    Kp 0-2 Quiet

    Faint aurora visible in photographs, mostly near the Arctic Circle. Best viewed in northern Iceland, Norway, or Canada.

    Kp 3-4 Active

    Bright auroras visible to the naked eye. Green colors become obvious. Good displays at zenith in Nordic countries.

    Kp 5-6 Storm

    Vibrant, dynamic displays with multiple colors. Red and purple hues appear. Aurora coronae likely. Memorable experiences.

    Kp 7-9 Severe Storm

    Exceptional displays visible at mid-latitudes (40-50°). Intense colors, rapid movement. Rare and spectacular events.
